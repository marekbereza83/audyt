# Pricing

Please refer to [Plans and Pricing table](https://api.immersal.com/pricing/) for details.

### Free License

To boost the development of Spatial AR applications, we offer a free license for developers. With a free account, you can use the Immersal Mapper application to map your small-scale space using up to 100 images—a very welcoming option for developers eager to quickly experience the Immersal development workflow. It is important to note, however, that the free license does not support the development of commercial projects, and starting from v1.18, we require the Immersal logo to be displayed throughout the AR experience. The logo package can be found in the [Immersal SDK Samples](https://github.com/immersal/immersal-sdk-samples) repository under `Assets/ImmersalSDK/Samples/ImmersalLogos`.

### Pro License

For professional developers or small studios, we offer a Pro license. In addition to increasing the image limit per map from 100 to 500, developers can use a variety of spatial mapping methods beyond Immersal Mapper, such as Polycam, panoramic cameras, Matterport, and advanced LiDAR systems from multiple brands. On the client side, users are not limited to smartphones or tablets—they can also use various types of AR/MR glasses. Most importantly, the Pro license allows developers to create commercial applications.

### Enterprise License

For developers looking to build augmented reality or localization applications for larger spaces, we offer an Enterprise license. With the Enterprise license, your development capabilities are greatly enhanced. Not only is there no limit on the number of images per map, but you can also stitch or align maps to cover areas on the scale of buildings, city blocks, or even entire cities. Moreover, you can flexibly deploy the Immersal server within your own private network, ensuring data security while seamlessly integrating advanced Immersal visual localization with your own solutions. In addition, you will benefit from dedicated, real-time development support to help advance your project.

If you want to discuss our enterprise licensing, contact us via our [Contact Form](https://immersal.com/contact-us) or email us at [sales@immersal.com](mailto:sales@immersal.com)
